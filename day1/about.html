About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture ideas, reminders, and tasks as they come to mind. It helps you keep important thoughts organized so you can focus on what matters next.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea in the New note field.
  2. Select Add Note to save it to your notes.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need a quick reminder.

Features

  • Capture notes quickly.
  • Keep reminders in one convenient list.
  • Use a simple, focused interface.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Enter Add the current note
Esc Clear the note field
